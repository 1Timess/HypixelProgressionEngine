## Authorized separation

- `knowledge.wikiUrl` comes from NEU WIKI_URL extraction; it has no Armor gameplay or acquisition use site.
- `npcSellPrice` is a canonical disposal value. Armor uses gross BUY quotes and zero ALREADY_OWNED acquisition, without resale proceeds. It remains in the catalog.
- `sources` entries hypixel/neu and strictly shaped NEU repository/branch/etag/downloadedAt records identify providers and snapshots. Raw snapshot identity still binds effect and inactive-replacement proofs before grouping. Unknown source structures fail closed.
- Only NEU crafting grids with all nine slots, string ingredients/counts, supported optional crafting type/output count, and exact output ID may leave the key. Armor does not execute recipes or compare crafting plans. Opaque recipe extensions, other providers, trade/forge records and malformed grids fail closed; this is not a blanket recipe-data deletion.

The projection retains rarity, material, category, requirements including their metadata, Dungeon flags/requirements/Gear Score/conversion/upgrades, tradeability, museum, gemstones and all metadata not closed by the existing field contract. Abilities/capabilities remain, and mechanicKey independently binds effects/dependencies. Complete concrete variant identity also remains in candidate groups. Unknown and known states never become interchangeable. Future canonical fields remain in the rest projection by default.

Object key order and unordered requirement/ability/source/recipe collections are normalized without changing canonical objects. Ordered lore, upgrade stages and gemstone slots keep their ordering. Unsupported objectives/contexts receive no new acquisition/provenance omission permission.
